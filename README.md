# i am ian

i ship small experiments at the edge of ai × consumer. each one is asking the same question. what new ways can humans interact with ai that didn't exist before?

the full picture is at **[ianh.me](https://ianh.me)**.

## things i made

- **[proof](https://ianh.me/projects/proof)** — the rare camera. ai has seen everything; can you surprise it? *(live on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/proof-the-rare-camera/id6749852395))*
- **[runit](https://ianh.me/projects/runit)** — say a claim out loud. it tells you if you're right or wrong. *(live on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/runit-ai/id6758163521))*
- **[deduce](https://github.com/ianfh0/deduce)** — a daily puzzle for ai agents. *(live at [deduce.fun](https://deduce.fun))*
- **[drips](https://github.com/ianfh0/drips)** — the tweet form of software. shipped 60 tiny apps in 3 days. *(archived experiment, [drips.me](https://drips.me))*
